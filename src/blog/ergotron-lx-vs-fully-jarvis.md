Stock monitor stands consume significant desktop depth while forcing your neck into static, downward-tilted viewing angles. Upgrading to a heavy-duty mechanical monitor arm is the single most impactful adjustment you can make to your daily workspace ergonomics.

In this comparison, we test two industry benchmarks: the **Ergotron LX** and the **Fully Jarvis Monitor Arm**. Both command premium price points, but they approach stability and cable management with distinct engineering philosophies.

## Build Quality & Weight Capacity

* **Ergotron LX:** Built on patented Constant Force technology, the LX uses a polished aluminum fluid-motion cylinder. It handles displays ranging from 7 to 25 pounds with effortless fingertip height adjustments.
* **Fully Jarvis:** Features a robust heavy-gauge steel construction designed to support ultra-wide and heavy monitors up to 30 pounds with minimal arm sag.

## Adjustment Range & Flexibility

When dialing in your dual or single-monitor setup, fluid articulation matters. 

* The **Ergotron LX** offers an impressive 13 inches of vertical lift and extends up to 25 inches horizontally, allowing you to push the display completely back against the wall when clearing your desk for sketching or paperwork.
* The **Fully Jarvis** excels in rigid positioning under heavy loads, maintaining rock-solid tension even when subjected to intense mechanical typing or standing desk height transitions.

## Verdict

Choose the **Ergotron LX** if you frequently adjust your monitor height and value butter-smooth fingertip articulation. Choose the **Fully Jarvis** if you are mounting a massive ultra-wide screen and prioritize maximum weight capacity and rigid stability.
